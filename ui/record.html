ready input level
Speak normally: the level bar should reach the middle. If it barely moves, pick another microphone above or raise the mic level in Windows Sound settings.

Notes for a clean take

  • Quiet room: no music, TV, fans or typing. Close the window.
  • 20–30 cm from the microphone, slightly off-axis to avoid pops. Don't touch the desk or laptop.
  • Start within 1 second of the countdown ending. The clone copies its timbre from the first 3 seconds, so make the first sentence your best, most natural one.
  • Talk, don't read: your normal speed, energy and expression. Flat reading makes a flat clone.
  • Keep a steady volume; no shouting, no whispering. Breathe normally; avoid pauses longer than a second.
  • The whole take trains the voice lock, so keep talking for the full length even if you finish the script (describe your day).
  • Headset mics are fine; laptop mics work if the room is quiet.
